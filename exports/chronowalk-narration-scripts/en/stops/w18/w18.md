[warm]   Which brings us to the one in the middle - and the most famous argument in the history of public art commissions. [thoughtful]   The result is the Fountain of the Four Rivers - Bernini's, 1651. An ancient obelisk shoulders the sky at its centre. [matter-of-fact]   The obelisk is a Roman fake: Domitian had it made in imitation of an Egyptian one, hieroglyphs and all, carved here in Rome. [warm]   The Ganges, for Asia, holds an oar - its course was long and navigable and known. The Danube, for Europe, reaches toward the papal coat of arms - the gesture of the loyal.
