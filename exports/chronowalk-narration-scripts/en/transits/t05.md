[thoughtful]  The marble left slowly. Not in one event - in a thousand individual decisions across fifteen centuries, each one reasonable to the person making it. A church needed a floor. A palace needed a column. What you're walking through now is the result of that arithmetic: what was too heavy to lift, too hard to cut, too dangerous to dismantle. [slow]  What you see... is what remained when everything portable was gone. The Via Sacra ran through all of it.
