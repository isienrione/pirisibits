[warm]  The emperor's approach - good choice. Your calves have been warned. [matter-of-fact]  Walk toward the triple arch standing alone at the piazza's edge. That's the Arch of Constantine... and it's hiding something in plain sight. Money was tight in 315 AD, so most of its carvings were pried off older monuments - other emperors' triumphs, their faces re-cut into Constantine's. [dry]  An emperor literally made of other emperors. Rome recycled everything... including glory. [quiet]  Hold the name. Inside the Forum there's a building he stole even more thoroughly... from a man he drowned.

[warm]  Now keep the arch on your left and turn down Via di San Gregorio - the long tree-lined avenue. The Palatine entrance opens on your right. [slow]  We take the hill first... the way power did.
