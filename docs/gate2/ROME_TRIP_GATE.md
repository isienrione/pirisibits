# Rome Trip 1 — GO / NO-GO Gate

Purpose: no money is spent on the first Rome field trip until every REQUIRED item below is green. The founder's eight proposed criteria were each evaluated, not auto-accepted.

## Evaluation of proposed criteria

| # | Proposed criterion | Verdict | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | B1 remote prototype reaches threshold | **NOT required** (demoted to "informative") | B1's *result* doesn't gate the trip — B3 (VPS) and B2 justify Rome on their own. Requiring B1 to *pass* would serialize the program and could delay a trip whose most valuable experiment (B3) is B1-independent. What IS required is that B1's flagship vantage **assets exist** (criterion 8) because B2/B5/AB reuse them. |
| 2 | B2 local desk-illusion convincing enough | **REQUIRED** | B2 is the trip's biggest build. If the illusion fails on a pre-shot backplate at a desk, it will not survive Roman sunlight. Concrete bar: internal panel (founder + 2 fresh eyes) rates the desk illusion ≥ 5/7 on TP1 ("could imagine the place in another era") — not a user study, a sanity gate. |
| 3 | B3 probe app fully instrumented | **REQUIRED — hardest gate** | B3 is the only experiment that *cannot* be run anywhere else and produces zero value without automatic logs. Bar: a full scripted measurement walk at a local site produces complete, parseable logs for all 3 providers with no manual note-taking. |
| 4 | X-A3 audio production-quality | **REQUIRED, amended** | Correct principle (Rome must test PLACE, not unfinished sound design) but scoped to whichever audio rung is actually going to Rome. If A3 was killed in Phase 1, the requirement transfers to the surviving rung (A2 or A1). Bar: the Rome audio cell passed its Phase 1 threshold *and* has had one polish pass. |
| 5 | HPS v0 frozen | **REQUIRED** | Non-negotiable. Changing the instrument mid-program destroys comparability between Phase 1 and Rome cells. Freeze = founder sign-off on `HPS_V0_FROZEN_INSTRUMENT.md`. |
| 6 | All logging automatic | **REQUIRED** | Field days are the scarcest resource in the program; manual logging burns them. Applies to B3 logs, HPS collection (forms), session metadata, and B2 calibration timing. |
| 7 | Protocol rehearsed locally | **REQUIRED** | One full dress rehearsal at the stand-in site: all planned Rome cells, real participants (n ≥ 3), timed. This is the cheapest insurance in the program — every field-study failure mode (consent flow, battery, transitions between cells, questionnaire timing) shows up here first. |
| 8 | Reconstruction assets locked | **REQUIRED, amended** | "Locked" = the 1–3 Forum vantages for B2/B5/AB are final and composited; *rough* assets are acceptable only for the B5 on-site variant (per founder's fidelity-independence directive). No art iteration in Rome. |

## Additional gates (added, not in founder list)
| 9 | Stop permissions & logistics confirmed | **REQUIRED** | Colosseum/Forum entry rules, filming/testing constraints, timed tickets, backup stops if access fails. A permissions surprise can zero a field day. |
| 10 | Recruitment plan for on-site participants | **REQUIRED** | Script + incentive for intercepting travelers (target n per cell), plus 2 brought testers as floor if intercept fails. |
| 11 | Trip data-loss protection | **REQUIRED** | Same-day log/footage backup routine; a lost phone must cost one day, not the trip. |

## Final gate (GO requires ALL):
2, 3, 4(amended), 5, 6, 7, 8(amended), 9, 10, 11. Item 1 is reported but non-blocking.

## Trip 1 abort/degrade rules (pre-declared)
- If B3 instrumentation breaks in the field: continue trip; B3 becomes Trip 2's anchor requirement.
- If weather/access kills a field day: priority order for remaining time is B3 > B2 > AB > A3-confirmation > ride-alongs (D2 map authoring, D4 walks, B4 capture).
- Trip 2 happens only if Trip 1 produces at least one PASS or one AMBIGUOUS-worth-iterating among {B2, AB, B3} — otherwise the program returns to desk (see decision tree).
