CHRONOWALK 2.0 — GATE 1
FRONTIER EXPERIENCE + FEASIBILITY EXPERIMENT DESIGN

Gate 0 is approved as a strong foundation, with two important founder corrections before proceeding.

==================================================
FOUNDER CORRECTION #1:
DO NOT MAKE AUDIO THE GATEKEEPER FOR SPATIAL/VISUAL INNOVATION
==================================================

Your E1 spatial-audio Presence Test is valuable and should remain in the experimental program.

However, I do NOT approve the statement:

"If audio craft alone can't manufacture presence, no camera trick will save the product."

That is an unsupported assumption.

ChronoWalk's potential breakthrough may come from the COMBINATION of:

- narrative
- physical location
- cinematic audio
- spatial audio
- historical reconstruction
- camera/computer vision
- AR/spatial computing
- interaction
- visual transformation

A visual/spatial reconstruction may create historical presence that audio alone cannot.

Therefore:

Do not make E1 a prerequisite for testing visual/spatial experiences.

Audio and spatial reconstruction experiments should proceed as PARALLEL discovery tracks.

ChronoWalk 2.0 must not prematurely become "an extraordinarily good audio guide."

We are explicitly searching for something potentially category-defining beyond conventional audio tours.

==================================================
FOUNDER CORRECTION #2:
DO NOT PREMATURELY CONVERGE ON SWIFTUI
==================================================

Your current SwiftUI-native leaning is a hypothesis, not a decision.

Do not optimize architecture around it yet.

The final architecture must emerge from the experience requirements and experiments.

We need to understand whether the best ChronoWalk requires:

- native SwiftUI
- React Native / Expo + native modules
- Flutter
- Kotlin Multiplatform
- shared domain/content core + native experience shells
- Unity or another 3D layer for selected experiences
- RealityKit / ARKit modules
- a hybrid architecture
- or another credible approach.

Remember:

iOS launches first.

Android follows.

Android must not require rebuilding the company.

But cross-platform convenience must also NOT cap the quality of the experience.

==================================================

DO NOT BUILD THE CONSUMER APP YET.

Design the experimental program that will tell us what ChronoWalk 2.0 SHOULD be before we commit to its architecture.

The experiments must answer:

"What creates the greatest believable historical presence on a real traveler, at a quality and production cost that can eventually support a global product?"

Use the Rome vertical slice:

- Pantheon
- Roman Forum
- Colosseum

==================================================
TRACK B — VISUAL / SPATIAL THRESHOLD
==================================================

This is equally important to Track A.

Prototype conceptually and technically compare:

B1. PREMIUM CINEMATIC THEN/NOW

Perfectly matched present/historical viewpoint.

Press, drag, hold or another interaction.

Potential:
- parallax
- depth layers
- subtle motion
- environmental sound
- historical overlays

B2. CAMERA-ASSISTED 2.5D RECONSTRUCTION

User raises camera.

App guides viewpoint alignment.

Historical reconstruction overlays the live scene using the minimum robust technology necessary.

Investigate:
- image matching
- feature matching
- homography
- segmentation
- depth estimation
- camera pose
- device orientation
- parallax
- lightweight geometry
- GPU compositing

One possible interaction remains:

"Hold to restore Rome."

But DO NOT assume this is the best interaction.

B3. TRUE ANCHORED AR

Investigate an actual spatial reconstruction using appropriate native spatial technologies.

Evaluate:
- visual anchors
- world anchors
- geospatial/VPS
- RealityKit / ARKit
- ARCore implications for Android
- occlusion
- tracking stability
- movement
- scale
- outdoor behavior

B4. PHOTOREAL SPATIAL RECONSTRUCTION

Investigate whether techniques involving:

- Gaussian splatting
- NeRF-derived assets
- photogrammetry
- authored 3D + neural rendering
- hybrid reconstructed geometry

can realistically represent a LOST historical environment rather than merely scanning what exists today.

B5. INVENT A FIFTH OPTION

Do not limit yourself to our ideas.

Propose at least one historical-reconstruction interaction that neither the current ChronoWalk nor this prompt has described.

It must be technically credible enough to prototype.

==================================================
THE THREE ROME TEST CASES
==================================================

Do not treat the three stops identically.

PANTHEON

Much of the structure survives.

Ask:
What does reconstruction reveal that the traveler cannot already see?

Could multiple eras, color, ritual, crowd, roof/interior treatment or spatial audio be more valuable than simply rebuilding geometry?

ROMAN FORUM

This is perhaps the strongest reconstruction test.

The traveler sees fragments and ruins.

Ask:
Can ChronoWalk restore the missing city around them convincingly enough that the ruins become legible?

This should aggressively test camera/spatial reconstruction.

COLOSSEUM

Test:
- monumental scale
- missing structures
- crowd
- arena/hypogeum relationships
- multiple viewpoints
- historical phases

Ask whether this deserves flagship true AR / spatial treatment.

==================================================
IMPORTANT DISTINCTION: PROTOTYPE VS PRODUCT
==================================================

We are allowed to use technical shortcuts in an experiment to answer a question.

We are NOT allowed to mistake the shortcut for production architecture.

Example:

If we can manually calibrate a Pantheon reconstruction to test whether the experience is magical, do that before building an automated city-wide calibration system.

Test the user value first.

Then engineer scale.

==================================================

- "I see this place differently now."

- "I would pay for this experience in another city."

Include:

- quantitative questions
- qualitative questions
- behavioral signals
- what NOT to measure
- how to avoid leading testers

Do not make a single arbitrary 70% threshold our sole decision criterion.
