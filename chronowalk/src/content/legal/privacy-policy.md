## 2. What data we collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may process:

- **Contact and order data** — your email address and a confirmation that a purchase was made (we receive this from Paddle; we do **not** receive your card number).
- **Location data** — your approximate or precise device location **while you are actively using a walking experience**, to power navigation and unlock nearby content. See Section 5.
- **Device and technical data** — device type, operating system, browser, language, and similar technical information.
- **Usage and analytics data** — how you interact with the Service (screens viewed, features used, events, and, if you consent, cookie-based analytics).
- **Diagnostic data** — crash reports and error logs to help us fix problems.
- **Support data** — the content of any message you send us and information needed to respond.

We do **not** knowingly collect special-category data, and we do not sell your personal data.

## 3. Why we use your data, and our legal basis

Under the GDPR we must have a legal basis for each use. These are ours:

| What we do | Why | Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6) |
|---|---|---|
| Deliver the experience you purchased and give you access | To perform our contract with you | **Contract** |
| Provide customer support and handle refund/technical requests | To perform our contract and respond to you | **Contract** / **Legitimate interests** |
| Provide navigation and unlock location-based content | To deliver the core feature you asked for | **Contract** (and **consent** for device location access — see Section 5) |
| Improve the Service, fix bugs, understand usage | To operate and improve a product you use | **Legitimate interests** (and **consent** for non-essential analytics cookies) |
| Detect and prevent fraud and abuse | To protect our business and users | **Legitimate interests** |
| Comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations | Because the law requires it | **Legal obligation** |
| Send optional updates (only if you opt in) | To keep interested users informed | **Consent** |

Where we rely on **legitimate interests**, we balance our interests against your rights, and you can object (see Section 9).

## 5. Location / GPS data

Location is central to how ChronoWalk works, so we want to be clear:

- We access your device location **only while you are actively using a walking experience**, to show your position, guide navigation, and unlock nearby content.
- Your browser/device will ask for permission first; you can **decline or revoke** location access at any time in your device or browser settings (though the experience will be limited without it).
- Where technically feasible, location is used **on your device** to drive the experience; we do not build location profiles of you, and **we do not sell location data**.
- We may process coarse, aggregated location signals to improve routes and content.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **correct** inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **delete** your data ("right to be forgotten");
- **restrict** or **object** to certain processing (including processing based on legitimate interests, and any direct marketing);
- **data portability** — receive your data in a portable format;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

To exercise any right, contact **[support@chronowalk.com]**. We will respond within the time limits required by law (generally one month under the GDPR). You also have the right to **lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority** (for EU users, the supervisory authority in your country; for the UK, the ICO). We'd appreciate the chance to resolve concerns first.
