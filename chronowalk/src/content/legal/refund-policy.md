ChronoWalk sells digital, self-guided historical walking experiences. This policy explains when refunds are available and how to request one. It sits alongside — and does not override — your mandatory statutory consumer rights or **Paddle's Refund Policy** (see Section 6).

---

## 1. Digital content and immediate access

Each ChronoWalk experience is **digital content** delivered immediately after purchase. Because access is granted right away, please read the product description and device requirements before buying.

---

## 2. EU / EEA / UK consumers — withdrawal right

If you are a consumer in the EU, EEA, or UK, you normally have a 14-day "cooling-off" right to withdraw from a purchase of digital content.

For digital content that is supplied **immediately**, this right can be lost once supply has begun — but only if, at checkout, you **expressly consent** to immediate access **and acknowledge that you therefore lose your right of withdrawal**. By starting a ChronoWalk experience after purchase, you confirm that consent and acknowledgement.

> Important for your checkout, not just this page: to actually rely on the above, your purchase flow (via Paddle) must capture that explicit consent + acknowledgement **before** access begins. If it doesn't, the withdrawal right can still apply. Confirm this checkbox/notice is present in your Paddle checkout.

This does not affect your other statutory rights if the content is faulty or not as described.

---

## 3. When we'll refund

We want you to have a good experience. We will generally approve a refund where:

- you were **charged more than once** or bought the **same experience twice** by mistake;
- a **verified technical fault on our side** prevented you from accessing or using the experience, and we could not resolve it;
- the experience **could not be delivered** due to a failure of the Service;
- the content is **faulty, or materially not as described**;
- a refund is otherwise **required by applicable consumer law**.

If something went wrong, contact us — we'd rather fix it or refund you than leave you unhappy.

---

## 4. When a refund may not be available

Outside of the situations above and your statutory rights, refunds may be declined where:

- you **changed your mind after substantially using** the experience;
- your device or browser **does not meet the published requirements** (please check these before buying);
- **GPS or signal problems are caused by your device, network, or surroundings** rather than a fault in the Service;
- you simply **did not finish** the experience for reasons unrelated to a fault.

We assess requests reasonably and in good faith, and always subject to your legal rights.

## 5. How to request a refund

You can request a refund through **Paddle**, our Merchant of Record, or by contacting us and we'll help:

**[support@chronowalk.com]**

To help us resolve it quickly, please include:

- your **order number** (from your Paddle receipt);
- the **email address** used at purchase;
- a short **description of the problem**.

---

## 6. How refunds work with Paddle

Because **Paddle is the Merchant of Record**, Paddle processes payments and issues refunds:

- Paddle reviews eligible requests and, where approved, refunds to your **original payment method**, typically **within 14 days of approval**.
- Paddle applies its own Refund Policy and reviews requests case by case; **Paddle's records do not override your statutory rights.**
- We work with Paddle on requests relating to ChronoWalk; where we agree to a refund, Paddle issues it on our behalf.

You can find Paddle's Buyer Terms and Refund Policy at paddle.com, and manage orders or contact Paddle support at paddle.net.
