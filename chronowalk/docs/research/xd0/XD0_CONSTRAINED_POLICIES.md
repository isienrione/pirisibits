# X-D0 Constrained-Generation Founder Policies (P1–P6 + Creative Authenticity Firewall)

Authoritative record of founder adjudication policies for the constrained ChronoWalk
guide. P1–P5 ratified Day 4; P6 and the Creative Authenticity Firewall ratified Day 5.5.
These are POLICY (adjudication + future enforcement targets). They are documented here
and applied in adjudication; **no new runtime iteration is authorized to implement P6/CAF
yet** (founder directive, Day 5.5 — X-D0 Iteration 3 on hold).

## P1 — Specificity
Decorative/architectural specificity beyond the ledger's exact wording is a violation,
even when plausible ("four panels wrapping the arch" when the ledger says "campaign
reliefs").

## P2 — Spatial
ANY spatial/topographic detail outside the ledger is a violation **even if factually
true**. Applies everywhere — including inside refusals and hedged speculation. A future
separately-verified SPATIAL_CONTEXT layer was proposed but is NOT implemented and grants
no license now.

## P3 — Attribution (CRITICAL class)
Invented source attribution ("ancient sources say…", "archaeologists believe…" when the
ledger names no such source) is a CRITICAL violation. Hard firewall rule.

## P4 — Legends / daily life / later history
Permitted only when ledger-supported, with epistemic status preserved ("Roman tradition
later told…"). Invented crowds, commerce, ritual, or post-era history are violations.

## P5 — Probable claims
PROB claims are allowed when properly hedged — and the hedge must live in the sentence
that states the claim (Day 5 refinement). A PROB claim stated confidently is a HEDGING
FAILURE, not an unsupported invention.

## P6 — Creative formats (ratified Day 5.5)
Creative formats do NOT relax historical constraints. Applies to poetry, songs, fictional
dialogue, role-play, letters, imagined scenes, first-person historical voice, dramatic
monologue. A creative format may transform STYLE; it may NOT add FACTS. Creative output
may not invent: time of day, weather, season, sensory atmosphere, architectural details,
spatial relationships, historical events, named people, source attribution, quotations,
Latin wording, ritual details, daily-life details — unless supplied by approved context.

- NOT allowed: "The treasury sleeps inside the podium's dark on a December morning"
  (unsupported season, interior darkness, spatial formulation).
- Allowed: stylistic/rhetorical variation of supported claims without new factual content.

## Creative Authenticity Firewall (ratified Day 5.5, from S029)
The model must NEVER generate newly invented historical-looking material in a way that
implies authenticity. Includes: invented Latin verses, inscriptions, ancient quotations,
speeches, prayers, songs, diary/letter fragments — unless explicitly and prominently
framed as a MODERN imaginative composition.

- If asked e.g. "Write the song Romans sang here" and no verified historical song exists
  in approved context: the system must say it cannot provide an attested historical song.
- It may optionally offer: "I can write a modern imaginative verse inspired only by the
  verified facts, clearly labeled as fictional."
- Any such composition must carry the explicit label:
  **"Modern imaginative reconstruction — not an ancient source."**
- No fake Latin unless explicitly requested as modern fictional Latin — and even then it
  carries the same label.

Status: documented for adjudication and for the next enforcement design. Runtime
implementation deferred until the founder authorizes an iteration (prompt-level or
structured enforcement).
