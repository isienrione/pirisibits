# ChronoWalk 2.0 — City Graph Architecture (Strategic Record)

**Status:** DOCUMENTATION ONLY. Founder-approved strategic direction, recorded per Day 3 partial-approval instruction. Nothing in this document authorizes any build, content generation, refactor, or commerce change.

**Date recorded:** 2026-08-08 (Day 3, Gate 2 / Phase 0)

---

## Approved strategic model

ChronoWalk Rome evolves from a single flagship vertical slice into a **content graph of city stops** served by **dynamic routes**.

### Scale expectations
- **21–25+ Anchor stops** expected for Rome.
- **~50+ total Rome stops** over time, across all classes.

### Stop classes (variable production depth by class)
| Class | Role | Production depth |
|---|---|---|
| **Anchor** | Must-see canonical sites; the backbone of any route | Full depth: verified claim ledger, historical verification pass, ART-tier reconstruction assets, full interaction (B1/Lantern-class), HPS-tested |
| **Signature** | High-value distinctive sites that differentiate routes | Intermediate depth; scoped per stop |
| **Discovery** | Hidden gems, small moments, connective surprises | Lightweight depth; narration-led, minimal or no reconstruction art |

### Routing model
- Stops form a **content graph**, not a fixed linear tour.
- Routes are **generated dynamically** over the graph.
- Route inputs: **geography** (where the visitor is / walking distances), **time available**, **interests**, and **context** (weather, opening hours, crowd, prior stops).
- Every generated route balances **must-see anchors PLUS hidden discoveries**.

### Commerce
- Simple customer-facing commerce **may remain** as-is. No change is authorized by this document.

### Sequencing principles (founder-ratified)
1. **Quality before stop count.** No scale-out while the flagship quality bar is unproven.
2. **Automated production pipeline before mass content generation.** The per-stop pipeline (ledger authoring → historical verification → constrained narration → asset production → QA) must be systematized and validated before any 50-stop content push. The Day 2–3 finding stands: human historical verification is the scaling bottleneck and must be engineered for, not bypassed.
