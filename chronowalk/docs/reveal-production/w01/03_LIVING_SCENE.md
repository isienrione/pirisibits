# w01 — 03 Living Scene

**Place:** `w01` Colosseum exterior  
**Goal:** OCCUPIED + FUNCTIONING arrival plaza — observed, not staged  
**Not:** Hollywood spectacle outside the amphitheatre  
**Founder seal:** Restrained human life **approved**; stalls only if defensible + subordinate (`00_FOUNDER_DECISIONS.md`)

---

## Desired feeling

“People are arriving at a working venue.”  
Anticipation, circulation, shade, stone, sky — not combat.

---

## Include (restrained)

| Category | Direction |
|----------|-----------|
| Approaching visitors | Small groups / pairs walking toward ground arches |
| Perimeter circulation | A few figures moving along the curve |
| Attendants | 1–2 near a primary entrance (non-theatrical) |
| Light commerce | **At most 1–2** modest stalls with pottery/baskets — background only; **omit if not historically defensible** |
| Fabric (still) | Partial velarium cloth + clothing folds — explain function, **not** a sealed giant roof |
| Atmosphere | Dust motes / soft haze optional; match Now daylight |
| Sun / shadow | **Preserve photo lighting:** light from **left**; arch interiors dark |

Density target: **~8–20 figures** visible in plaza — enough for scale, not a mob.

---

## Explicitly reject

- Gladiators fighting **outside**
- Charging horses / animal hunts on plaza
- Giant imperial procession without evidence
- Everyone in identical white togas
- Fantasy banners / flags with invented emblems
- Explosions, fire, blood, cinematic smoke plumes
- Exaggerated orange “ancient Rome” color grade
- Impossible crowd density packing the entire plaza
- Random **pseudo-Latin** signs or plaques
- Modern tourists, phones, metal crowd barriers in Then
- Camera flyovers / orbiting shots / recomposition
- Giant fully closed velarium roof dominating the façade

---

## Composition notes (mobile)

- Keep life in **lower third** (plaza) so façade registration stays clean.  
- Avoid large foreground figures that occlude A1–A6 anchors.  
- Thumb/hold zone: lower-center; do not place critical “wow” only under typical thumb cover.  
- Focal story: **jagged ruin → complete outer ring** at mid façade; life supports, does not compete.

---

## Motion (deferred)

**Founder seal:** Do **not** produce optional motion until the final Then still passes registration / historical / visual QA.

When later authorized: cloth ripple, slow walk cycles, dust only — no cuts, zooms, orbit, or combat loops.
