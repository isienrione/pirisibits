# w01 — Founder decisions (sealed)

**Sealed:** Day 3D pre-production approval  
**Scope:** `w01` Colosseum Living Postcard production package  
**Status:** Binding for this iteration — update docs, not memory

These decisions supersede open “judgment needed” items in earlier pre-prod drafts where they conflict.

---

## Camera

1. **`modern-poster.jpg` is the permanent w01 camera lock for this iteration.**  
2. **Then must preserve this exact camera and composition.**  
3. **No camera recomposition** (no new angle, lens, height, crop-as-reframe, or orbit).

## Velarium

4. **Partial / deployed enough to explain function.**  
5. **Do not** create a giant, visually dominant, fully closed roof that overpowers the façade.

## Life & commerce

6. **Restrained human life is approved** (arrival / circulation / attendants per `03_LIVING_SCENE.md`).  
7. **1–2 modest peripheral stalls may be used only if historically defensible and visually subordinate.**

## Light & text

8. **Preserve the real photograph’s lighting direction/logic** (sun from left; dark arch interiors).  
9. **No invented / pseudo-Latin or uncertain reconstructed text.** Prefer omit.

## Delivery sequence

10. **Still-first remains mandatory.**  
11. **Do NOT produce optional motion** until the final Then still passes registration / historical / visual QA.  
12. **Do not award runtime `revealTier = flagship`** until `06_QA_SCORECARD.md` fully PASSes.
