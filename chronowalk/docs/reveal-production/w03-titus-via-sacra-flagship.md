## A. Experience intent

The Arch stays spatially recognizable while the **Via Sacra and surrounding Roman urban fabric become legible and inhabited**.

Central idea: **the Forum was not an archaeological park — it was a city.**

The road becoming a functioning road may matter more than merely polishing the Arch.

## B. Exact viewpoint

- Traveler at Arch of Titus on the Sacra Via approach (geofence).

- Portrait mobile framing.

## C. Registration anchors

| Class | Content |
