# PRODUCT_THESIS.md
ChronoWalk 2.0 — Gate 0 · August 2026
Sources: Founder Playbook v1.0 (ES, July 2026), Replit Master Pack, Gate 0 brief.

## The one-sentence thesis

**ChronoWalk exists to close the Presence Gap — the distance between what a traveler physically sees in a city and what they can imagine, understand, and feel about it — by making the invisible layer of history perceptible in the exact place and moment where it happened.**

This is not an audioguide, a city guide, or a travel app. It is a new category the founder names the **Historical Immersion Platform**: the shift from *sightseeing* to *city understanding*.

## The problem (from the Playbook, restated)

1. **Cities carry more meaning than travelers can perceive.** The history is not hidden; it is simply never revealed in the right place, at the right moment, in the right form.
2. **Existing formats fail the moment of presence.** Guidebooks are read before or after; audioguides are enumerative and enciclopedic; group tours remove freedom; AR gimmicks serve the tech, not the story.
3. **The traveler's trip is one of the highest-value moments of their life** — emotionally and financially — and it is systematically underserved by products that treat information delivery as the job, when the actual job is *transformation of perception*.

## What the product must do (job to be done)

> "Make me feel that the past exists again in the place where I am standing — and let me walk away understanding this city in a way I could not before."

The measurable expression of this is the **Historical Presence Score**: the intensity with which a person feels the place "came alive" — combining surveys, behavior, completion, scene repetition, shares, and comments. It is the editorial North Star metric, never a mechanical score.

## Product invariants (never lose these)

From the Master Pack hierarchy (Playbook > Pack > founder decisions > legacy artifacts > agent preference):

1. **The city is the protagonist.** Not the app, not the tech, not the narrator. Every interface decision defers to the place.
2. **Historical rigor is non-negotiable.** Every claim is classified (accepted fact / probable interpretation / hypothesis / reconstruction / legend / dramatized device) with documented sources and confidence. Emotion is never built on falsehood.
3. **Traveler freedom.** Self-paced, non-linear, interruption-tolerant. The traveler owns their walk; the product adapts to them, never the reverse.
4. **Quality before scale.** Nothing publishes below the 15-point standard; the 80% Rule gates every new city. One extraordinary city beats three mediocre ones.
5. **Technology serves the story.** Frontier tech is welcome only when it deepens presence. "We will not add AI because it is fashionable" applies equally to AR.

## The business thesis

- **Bundle-first pricing (~$12–17)**, direct-sale margins, Merchant of Record for global tax handling in early stages.
- **Rome is the laboratory, not the market.** The real product is a *repeatable system* for producing extraordinary city experiences — methodology, pipeline, Knowledge Graph, quality gates — validated in Rome, proven repeatable with city #2.
- **Travel Lifetime Value** is the growth engine: a connected catalog (Rome → Florence → Venice → Paris…) that accompanies a traveler's whole traveling life, not isolated city purchases.
- **Frugal, founder-financed, systems-before-hires.** Automation and documentation over headcount; the moat is process + Knowledge Graph + brand + reviews, never one specific technology.

## Why now (2026)

- On-device AI (Apple Foundation Models framework, on-device segmentation/depth) makes context-aware, offline-tolerant narrative feasible without cloud dependence at every step.
- City-scale visual positioning (ARCore Geospatial VPS, Niantic Spatial VPS2) has matured from demo to production API — precise outdoor anchoring in major tourist cities is now *practical frontier*, not speculation.
- Gaussian splatting reached real-time mobile rendering (Mobile-GS, MetalSplatter et al.), making photoreal historical reconstruction on a phone plausible for the first time.
- AI voice (multilingual, emotionally directed narration) collapses the cost of studio-quality audio across languages — but the Playbook's pipeline rule stands: research and editorial judgment remain human-led; AI drafts, humans decide.

## What success looks like at Gate 9

A traveler stands in the Roman Forum, holds up their phone (or just their ears), and for a moment the place *returns*. They finish the walk and say the sentence the founder wrote as the legacy: **"I had never understood a city that way."** And behind that single moment sits a documented, repeatable, city-agnostic system ready to do it again in Florence.
