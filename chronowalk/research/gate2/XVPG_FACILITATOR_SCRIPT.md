# X-VPG Facilitator Script (Day 5.5 — identical for every participant)

Rules baked into this script: no condition is named (only "the first/next version"); no
mention of AI; no hypothesis, no praise of any interaction; identical transition language
between cells; participant is told to behave as if standing at the real place and to
interact naturally without "solving" the interface. Read verbatim. Do not improvise.

The facilitator's session sheet contains only participant IDs and order codes (O1–O6);
the order key maps codes to neutral version labels A/B/C. The facilitator does not know
which label is which concept.

---

## 1. Welcome (once, before anything)

> "Thanks for helping today. You'll try three versions of an experience about a historical
> place, one after another. After each one, you'll answer a short questionnaire on this
> device. There are no right or wrong answers, and nothing you do is being graded — we're
> testing the versions, not you."

## 2. Standardized prototype notice (once, before the first cell — exact wording)

> "Before you begin: you are about to try early prototype reconstructions of a historical
> place. They were created to test how historical information is presented, and some
> visual details are intentionally simplified. As you use each one, focus on how well it
> helps you understand and imagine the place — not on artistic polish."

## 3. Framing (once, immediately after the notice)

> "For each version, imagine you are standing at the real place — on a terrace in Rome,
> looking out over the Roman Forum. Behave as you would there: look around, take your
> time. You can touch and interact with the screen naturally — there's nothing to figure
> out or solve, and you can't break anything."

## 4. Per-cell procedure (identical for every cell)

1. Open the version for this participant's next label (A/B/C per the order key) using the
   facilitator URL for that label. Hand the phone over screen-on, already loaded.
2. Say EXACTLY (same sentence for first/second/third — only the word changes):
   > "Here is the [first / next / last] version. Take about a minute and a half. I'll let
   > you know when time is up."
3. Start the 90-second timer. Do not speak during exposure unless the participant asks a
   direct technical question; if they do, answer only: "However feels natural is fine."
4. At time, say:
   > "Thank you. Please answer the questionnaire for this version now, before we talk."
5. Give the questionnaire (HPS form, participant enters ID + version label shown on the
   facilitator sheet). No conversation until submitted.
6. Washout: 2 minutes. Neutral break — offer water, look away from the device. Do not
   discuss any version.
7. Repeat from step 1 for the next cell.

## 5. After the third cell (triangulation block)

> "Last two questions. If you could keep only ONE of the three versions for a real trip
> to this place, which one — the first, second, or third you tried? … And in one
> sentence: why?"

Record the forced choice (by position, facilitator maps to label later) and the verbatim
reason. Then:

> "That's everything for today — thank you. We may send you one short question tomorrow."

(+24h probe, sent next day, verbatim: "Without looking anything up — what do you remember
most from yesterday's session?" Blind-scored place-vs-app later.)

## 6. Prohibitions (facilitator)

- Never name or describe the versions ("the slider one", "the lantern") in front of the
  participant, even after the session.
- Never react to a participant's opinion (no "yes, most people say that").
- Never show or discuss any scores.
- If a version fails technically: note it, restart it once; if it fails again, mark the
  cell VOID on the sheet and continue with the remaining cells.
