## 1. Hypothesis
At least one interaction concept (B1 layered cinematic or B5 Lantern) achieves
Δ ≥ +0.7 (core HPS or HL subscale) vs the baseline placard-style control using rough
ART-0 assets. (Matrix R1.6: decides whether the concepts deserve ART-1 investment.)

## 2. Cells
- **Control (C):** flattened binary Then↔Now of the shared reconstruction composite; parallax 0.
- **B1:** layered cinematic reveal (slider crossfade + banded depth parallax) of the same composite.
- **B5:** Lantern — draggable circular reveal of the same composite.
All three are modes of one component (`B1Shell.tsx`); neutral labels A/B/C per
`experiments/xvpg/out/label_map.json` (analyst-only).

## 3. Constants (held identical across cells)
Reconstruction content (single masked-band stack of one backdrop), source imagery,
resolution, device, viewport, exposure duration, narration (A1 track — same script,
voice, timing, loudness, autoplay-on-entry — in all cells or none), prototype notice,
facilitator script, questionnaire, washout.

## 4. Manipulated variable
Interaction concept ONLY (how the identical reconstruction is revealed).

## 5. Prototype notice
Final wording in `XVPG_FACILITATOR_SCRIPT.md` §2 — once, before the first cell,
identical for all participants; no AI mention, no cell named, no hypothesis revealed.

## 6. Participant instructions
`XVPG_FACILITATOR_SCRIPT.md` — read verbatim; identical transition language; behave as
if at the real place; interact naturally, nothing to solve.

## 7. Randomization
Balanced 3-condition orders (ABC/BCA/CAB/ACB/CBA/BAC), extended cyclically for N>6.
Generated by `experiments/xvpg/randomize.mjs`; session sheet stores participant IDs +
order codes only (no condition names reach the facilitator).

## 8. Exposure timing
90 seconds per cell, timer identical, facilitator silent during exposure.

## 9. Washout timing
2 minutes neutral break between cells; no discussion of any version.

## 10. HPS instrument
`HPS_V0_FROZEN_INSTRUMENT.md` (frozen; wording and scoring untouched), administered
immediately after each cell via the mobile form
(`mockups/xvpg-hps/XvpgHpsForm`): 8 core items + IB + AC + TR + NV1, 7-point,
randomized within blocks; no computed scores shown to participants.

## 11. Comprehension test
4 multiple-choice items on ledger-supported stimulus content (arch purpose, Saturn
columns, Basilica Julia function, Maxentius newness), embedded in the form; reported as
% correct per cell (legibility triangulation).

## 12. Attention-conflict veto (automated)
`experiments/xvpg/analyze.mjs`: a condition CANNOT pass, whatever its HPS, if
mean AC-raw > 4.5 OR ΔAC vs control ≥ +1.5 → verdict ITERATE (attention design).
The veto outranks every success threshold.

## 13. Novelty interpretation rule (automated)
NV1 excluded from all scores; reported per cell. ΔHPS ≥ +0.7 with NV1 ≥ 5.5 and
ΔHL < +0.4 → flagged **NOVELTY-DOMINANT RESULT — not a pass**. High HPS + high novelty +
no legibility gain, elevated attention conflict, or significantly higher interaction
burden blocks graduation (founder directive Day 5). Second-exposure rule: a passing cell
must retain ≥85% of its core-HPS delta on second exposure before further investment.

## 14. Pass / iterate / kill thresholds (signal bars, not product pass bars)
- **GO for ART-1:** either concept Δ ≥ +0.7 (core or HL) at E1/E2 with AC/IB clean
  (IB-raw ≤ 3.5 co-gate for B5-class).
- **Ambiguous (+0.4 to +0.7):** one iteration of interaction (not art), re-test.
- **STOP art spend:** both concepts < +0.4 → no ART-1 commission; pivot audio-led.

## 15. N
Min N = 6; preferred N = 8–10 (current randomization sheet: N=10).

## 16. Evidence level interpretation
Min-N → E1–E2; preferred-N with counterbalancing → E2 (directional). Any cell core-HPS
SD > 2.0 → that cell capped at E1 (matrix rule, automated in analysis). No E3 claims
from desk sessions; Rome cells required for E3–E4.

## 17. Known residual confounds (accepted, documented)
1. Lantern novelty — intrinsic; measured via NV1 + interpretation gate, not eliminated.
2. B1 parallax richness — inseparable from the concept itself.
3. Lantern partial-view: equal information ACCESS, potentially different uptake — part of
   the phenomenon under test; noted when reading HL/comprehension.
4. AI-generated backdrop inaccuracies — condition-neutral; only between-condition
   differences interpreted.
5. Interaction-burden asymmetry — measured (IB), co-gated.

## 18. Session record format
One JSONL line per (participant, cell) as specified in `analyze.mjs` header; forced
choice + one-line reason and +24h probe recorded on the facilitator sheet.
